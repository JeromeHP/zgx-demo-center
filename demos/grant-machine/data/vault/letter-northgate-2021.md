## Letter of Support for Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers

**Date:** March 15, 2021

**To:**
Dr. Renee Okafor
Dean of Workforce and Technical Programs
Harrow Creek Community College
400 College Drive
Harrow Creek, MS 38746

**Re: Support for NSF ATE Proposal HCCC-FICT-ATE-2021**

Dear Dr. Okafor,

On behalf of Northgate Precision Castings, I am writing to express our strongest endorsement of Harrow Creek Community College’s proposal for the "Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers" project, submitted to the National Science Foundation’s Advanced Technological Education (ATE) program. As a leading employer in our region’s manufacturing sector, Northgate has identified a critical shortage of skilled technicians capable of operating and maintaining advanced mechatronic systems. We believe this initiative is not only timely but essential for the economic vitality of our four-county service area.

Northgate Precision Castings is deeply committed to bridging the gap between academic training and industrial application. Over the past decade, we have observed that traditional vocational pathways often fall short in addressing the interdisciplinary nature of modern automation, which requires proficiency in mechanical engineering, electronics, and computer programming simultaneously. The "Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers" proposal directly addresses this deficit by proposing a comprehensive curriculum that aligns with industry standards. We are eager to partner with HCCC to ensure that our local workforce is equipped with the precise technical competencies required in today’s high-tech manufacturing environment.

Our support for this project is grounded in our desire to cultivate a talent pipeline that benefits both our organization and the broader rural community. By collaborating with Dr. Okafor, Marcus Lindqvist, and the faculty at HCCC, we aim to develop training modules that reflect real-world challenges faced by manufacturers like Northgate. We anticipate that this partnership will lead to the creation of new course materials and potentially dual-credit agreements with local high schools, thereby expanding access to technical education for first-generation students and those from underrepresented backgrounds in STEM fields.

We recognize that the success of such an ambitious program relies heavily on active industry engagement. Northgate Precision Castings pledges to contribute to this effort by providing subject matter expertise, facilitating site visits for students, and participating in advisory board meetings to ensure curriculum relevance. We are confident that the proposed outcomes—including the completion of 120 students and a robust placement rate—will be achieved through this collaborative approach.

The integration of mechatronics into our local educational infrastructure will not only enhance employability but also drive innovation within rural manufacturing sectors. By supporting this proposal, Harrow Creek Community College demonstrates a proactive commitment to workforce development that resonates with employers who are struggling to find qualified candidates. We look forward to working closely with the project team to monitor progress and ensure that the training provided meets the evolving needs of our industry.

Thank you for your leadership in this endeavor. Northgate Precision Castings is proud to stand behind this proposal and supports its immediate approval.

Sincerely,

**Thomas J. Halloway**
Plant Manager
Northgate Precision Castings
