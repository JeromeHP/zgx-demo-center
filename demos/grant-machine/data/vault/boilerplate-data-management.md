## Data Management and Sharing Plan

Harrow Creek Community College (HCCC) is committed to ensuring that all data generated under this award are managed, preserved, and shared in accordance with the National Science Foundation’s Data Management and Sharing Policy. As a public two-year institution serving four rural counties in the Mid-South, HCCC recognizes its responsibility to maintain rigorous standards for data integrity, privacy, and accessibility. This plan outlines the strategies for managing sensitive student records, securing proprietary curriculum materials, and openly sharing evaluation instruments and non-sensitive educational resources to maximize the impact of the Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP) project.

### Data Types and Management Strategies

The SATP project will generate two primary categories of data: sensitive personal information and open educational assets.

**Sensitive Data:** The project involves the collection of enrollment, completion, and employment placement data for participants in the Mechatronics and Industrial Automation tracks. This includes personally identifiable information (PII) such as student names, addresses, social security numbers, and academic records. To manage this data, HCCC will adhere strictly to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). All PII will be de-identified before being used for internal analysis or external reporting. Data storage will occur on HCCC’s secure, encrypted server infrastructure, with access restricted to authorized personnel including Project Director Dr. Renee Okafor, Co-Principal Investigator Marcus Lindqvist, and the designated data manager within the Office of Institutional Research. No PII will be shared externally without explicit, written consent from participants or as required by law.

**Non-Sensitive Data:** The project will produce curriculum modules, lab exercises, assessment instruments, and program evaluation reports. These materials are intended to support workforce development in rural manufacturing sectors. HCCC will maintain a centralized digital repository for these assets, ensuring version control and documentation of metadata. This repository will serve as the primary source for sharing open-access materials with other educational institutions, industry partners, and NSF ATE project teams.

### Security and Compliance Measures

HCCC’s IT security protocols align with federal standards for protecting educational data. All devices used to access project data must comply with HCCC’s acceptable use policy, including multi-factor authentication and regular password updates. The Director of Grants and Sponsored Programs, Dana Whitfield, will oversee compliance audits to ensure that all data handling procedures meet institutional and federal requirements. Additionally, the evaluation team from Bluestem Evaluation Group, led by Dr. Priya Raman, will receive de-identified datasets only, ensuring that no PII is exposed during the analysis of program outcomes such as placement rates and skill acquisition metrics.

### Data Sharing and Dissemination

HCCC adopts an open-access approach for non-sensitive project outputs to foster collaboration and scalability within the Advanced Technological Education community.

**Open Access Curriculum:** All developed curriculum materials, including syllabi, lab manuals, and digital learning objects created under this award, will be released under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. These resources will be deposited in the NSF ATE Central Repository and HCCC’s institutional open-access portal upon completion of each module development cycle. This ensures that educators across the Mid-South and beyond can adapt these materials to local industry needs without restrictive licensing barriers.

**Evaluation Instruments:** Standardized evaluation instruments, such as pre- and post-assessment surveys for student skill acquisition, will be made publicly available in a machine-readable format. This transparency allows other ATE projects to replicate or adapt our assessment methods, enhancing the comparability of outcomes across similar workforce development initiatives.

**Limited Access Data:** In cases where aggregated data may inadvertently identify small subgroups (e.g., fewer than five participants), HCCC will suppress or aggregate such data points to prevent re-identification. Any request for access to de-identified datasets beyond those shared openly must be submitted in writing to Dr. Okafor and approved by the HCCC Institutional Review Board if human subjects research components are involved.

By implementing these robust management and sharing practices, Harrow Creek Community College ensures that the Smart Automation Technician Pathway not only delivers immediate workforce benefits but also contributes lasting, open educational resources to the national STEM education community.
