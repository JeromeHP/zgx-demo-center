## Professional Background and Academic Credentials

Dr. Renee Okafor serves as the Dean of Workforce and Technical Programs at Harrow Creek Community College (HCCC), a public two-year institution serving four rural counties in the Mid-South. With a career dedicated to bridging the gap between academic training and industrial demand, Dr. Okafor holds advanced degrees in Adult and Higher Education Administration alongside technical certifications in Systems Engineering. Her leadership is defined by a commitment to equitable access for first-generation students, who comprise 41% of HCCC’s enrollment, and low-income learners, with Pell Grant recipients representing 52% of the student body.

Dr. Okafor’s administrative philosophy centers on collaborative curriculum design that responds directly to regional economic shifts. Prior to her current role, she directed several certificate programs focused on advanced manufacturing, where she established robust advisory boards comprising local industry leaders. Her expertise in program development has been instrumental in positioning HCCC as a critical talent pipeline for the region’s growing automation sector.

## Relevant Experience and Project Leadership

As Principal Investigator for the proposed Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP), Dr. Okafor brings extensive experience in managing federally funded workforce initiatives. She previously served as the PI for *Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers* (Award ID: HCCC-FICT-ATE-2021), a three-year National Science Foundation Advanced Technological Education grant active from September 2021 through August 2025. This prior award, totaling $598,112, successfully engaged 11 industry partners and resulted in the development of three new technical courses.

Under Dr. Okafor’s stewardship, the prior project achieved a 78% placement rate among the 61 respondents who completed employment surveys, demonstrating strong labor market alignment despite falling slightly short of the 120-completer target (achieving 84 completers). Her ability to secure and maintain partnerships with key regional employers—including Kessler Fluid Systems, Tri-County Packaging Cooperative, and Northgate Precision Castings—underscores her capacity to drive project engagement.

Dr. Okafor collaborates closely with Co-Principal Investigator Marcus Lindqvist, Chair of Mechatronics and Industrial Automation, to ensure technical rigor in the SATP curriculum. Together, they have leveraged lessons from the prior award to design a more streamlined pathway targeting 150 completers over three years. Dr. Okafor’s strategic oversight ensures that HCCC meets its institutional goals while providing students with credentials that lead directly to high-demand careers in smart automation and industrial systems. Her track record of securing industry letters of support and aligning curricula with employer needs positions her as a highly effective leader for the SATP initiative.
