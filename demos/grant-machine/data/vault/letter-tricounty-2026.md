## Letter of Collaboration: Tri-County Packaging Cooperative

**Date:** August 20, 2026

**To:**
Dr. Renee Okafor
Dean of Workforce and Technical Programs
Harrow Creek Community College (HCCC)
Four Rural Counties in the Mid-South

**From:**
Elias Thorne
Director of Operations
Tri-County Packaging Cooperative

Dear Dr. Okafor,

On behalf of Tri-County Packaging Cooperative, I am writing to formally express our enthusiastic support for the Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP) project led by Harrow Creek Community College. As a key industrial partner serving the regional manufacturing sector, we recognize that the transition toward advanced automation requires a workforce equipped with precise technical skills and adaptive problem-solving abilities. We are committed to partnering with HCCC to ensure this initiative meets the evolving needs of our industry.

### Strategic Alignment and Industry Need

Our cooperative operates at the intersection of traditional packaging mechanics and modern robotic integration. The previous success of HCCC’s *Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers* (2021–2025) demonstrated the institution's capacity to deliver high-quality workforce training, achieving a 78% placement rate among respondents. Building on that foundation, the SATP project addresses an urgent gap in our local labor market. We require technicians who can maintain, troubleshoot, and optimize automated systems rather than merely operate them. The proposed three-year pathway, with a target of 150 completers, aligns directly with our strategic goal to upskill the regional talent pool and reduce downtime associated with new equipment installations.

### Commitment of Resources

To demonstrate our tangible investment in this collaboration, Tri-County Packaging Cooperative commits to the following support mechanisms for the SATP project:

1.  **Equipment Donations:** We will provide a suite of industry-standard automated packaging modules and sensor arrays valued at over $50,000. These assets will be installed within HCCC’s technical labs by the start of the academic year in July 2027, ensuring students engage with current technology from day one. This equipment includes legacy control units for reverse-engineering exercises and modern PLC interfaces for contemporary programming instruction.

2.  **Advisory Committee Participation:** We pledge to appoint a senior representative to serve on the SATP Project Advisory Committee. Our nominee will participate in quarterly meetings to review curriculum relevance, advise on competency standards, and facilitate site visits for students. This engagement ensures that instructional content remains synchronized with real-world operational demands.

3.  **Internship and Hiring Pipeline:** While not strictly a financial contribution, we commit to offering priority consideration for internships and entry-level positions to SATP graduates. Our human resources team will work closely with Dean Okafor and Chair Marcus Lindqvist to structure these opportunities, aiming for high placement rates that reflect the project’s success metrics.

### Conclusion

Tri-County Packaging Cooperative views this collaboration as a vital component of our long-term sustainability strategy. By investing in the education of future technicians, we are securing the operational resilience of our own facilities and contributing to the economic vitality of the four rural counties served by HCCC. We look forward to working with Dr. Okafor, Mr. Lindqvist, and the broader project team to bring this pathway to fruition.

Please accept this letter as formal documentation of our support for the SATP grant application. Should you require further details regarding the equipment specifications or our advisory representative’s credentials, please do not hesitate to contact my office directly.

Sincerely,

*(Signature)*

Elias Thorne
Director of Operations
Tri-County Packaging Cooperative
