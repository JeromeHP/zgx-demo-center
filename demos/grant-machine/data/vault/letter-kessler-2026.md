## Letter of Collaboration

**Date:** August 12, 2026

**To:**
Dr. Renee Okafor
Dean of Workforce and Technical Programs
Harrow Creek Community College (HCCC)
Four rural counties in the Mid-South

**From:**
Elias Thorne
Plant Manager
Kessler Fluid Systems

**Re: Letter of Collaboration for the Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP)**

Dear Dr. Okafor,

On behalf of Kessler Fluid Systems, I am pleased to provide this formal letter of collaboration in support of the Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP) project at Harrow Creek Community College. As a key industrial partner in our region, we recognize the critical need for a highly skilled workforce capable of managing next-generation automation systems. Our review of the SATP proposal indicates that it aligns precisely with our operational requirements and long-term human capital strategy.

Kessler Fluid Systems has previously benefited from the strong talent pipeline established by HCCC’s prior NSF ATE-funded initiative, *Mechatronics Pathways for Rural Manufacturers*. While that project concluded in August 2025 with 84 completers, we remain committed to deepening our engagement with the college. The transition to the SATP represents a vital evolution in our local manufacturing ecosystem, moving beyond traditional mechatronics toward integrated smart automation solutions. We are eager to support this progression by integrating our specific technical needs into the curriculum and providing real-world application opportunities for students.

In accordance with the commitments outlined in the SATP proposal, Kessler Fluid Systems formally agrees to the following terms of collaboration:

1.  **Internship Hosting:** We commit to hosting up to six (6) SATP interns annually during their junior and senior years. These internships will be structured to provide hands-on experience with programmable logic controllers (PLCs), industrial IoT sensors, and automated assembly lines. Our facility in the service area is equipped with modern infrastructure that mirrors current industry standards, ensuring students gain relevant, transferable skills.

2.  **Hiring Commitment:** Upon successful completion of the SATP program, Kessler Fluid Systems pledges to prioritize qualified graduates for full-time employment within our production and maintenance departments. We aim to hire at least three (3) SATP completers per cohort, contingent upon performance evaluations during their internship periods. This commitment supports the project’s goal of improving regional placement rates and reducing the skills gap in rural manufacturing sectors.

3.  **Advisory Support:** Our senior engineers will participate in periodic advisory board meetings to ensure that training modules remain aligned with technological advancements in fluid systems and automation control. We believe that this feedback loop is essential for maintaining the relevance and rigor of the HCCC curriculum.

The SATP project’s target of 150 completers over three years represents a significant opportunity to stabilize our local workforce. By partnering with HCCC, Kessler Fluid Systems demonstrates its dedication to educational excellence and community development. We are confident that this collaboration will yield mutual benefits, fostering a new generation of technicians who are prepared to meet the demands of modern industrial environments.

We look forward to working closely with you, Dr. Okafor, and your team, including Chair Marcus Lindqvist, to ensure the success of this initiative. Please do not hesitate to contact me directly if you require further details regarding our facilities or hiring protocols.

Sincerely,
