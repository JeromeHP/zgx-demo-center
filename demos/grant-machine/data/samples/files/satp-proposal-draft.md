# Smart Automation Technician Pathway (SATP)

Submitted to the National Science Foundation, Advanced Technological Education
programme (NSF 24-584), Track 2 — Projects.

## Project Summary

Harrow Creek Community College proposes the Smart Automation Technician Pathway
(SATP), a three-year project building a regional technician pipeline for smart
automation and semiconductor-adjacent manufacturing across our four-county service
area. SATP will serve 400 students and place 95% of completers into high-wage
regional employment within six months of graduation.

Harrow Creek is the leading provider of advanced manufacturing education in the
state. Our approach is transformative and will revolutionize technician education
nationally, establishing a model that peer institutions will adopt.

**Intellectual Merit.** SATP advances the field by integrating industrial automation,
metrology and cleanroom practice into a stackable credential sequence.

**Broader Impacts.** The project serves a rural, high-poverty region with limited
access to technical careers.

## Results from Prior NSF Support

Harrow Creek held NSF ATE award HCCC-FICT-ATE-2021, "Mechatronics Pathways for Rural
Manufacturers" ($598,112, 2021–2025, including a one-year no-cost extension).

The project exceeded all targets. We graduated 120 completers against a target of
120, achieved outstanding employer satisfaction, and placed graduates at a rate well
above regional norms. Three new courses were developed and eleven industry partners
engaged. The external evaluation confirmed the project met every stated objective.

## Project Description

### Goals and Objectives

SATP will develop six new courses spanning industrial automation, applied metrology,
cleanroom practice and process control. Faculty will complete an industry externship
at a regional partner facility.

### Curriculum and Work-Based Learning

Students will progress through a stackable sequence culminating in an industry
credential. Work-based learning placements will be arranged with partner employers.

### Partnerships

Industry partners committed to this project include Northgate Precision Castings,
Kessler Fluid Systems, and Tri-County Packaging Cooperative. All partners have
provided letters of commitment describing internship placements and advisory roles.

### Broadening Participation

The project will recruit students from underrepresented groups across the service
area and support their progression to completion.

## Evaluation Plan

An external evaluator will assess the project annually and produce reports. We will
track enrollment, completion, and placement. The evaluation will demonstrate the
project's success and document its impact on the regional workforce.

## Sustainability

Following the award period, the college will sustain the pathway through its
operating budget and continued employer engagement.
