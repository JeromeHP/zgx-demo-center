# Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System (SSI)

A consortium proposal submitted by the Sablecreek State University System on behalf of
its four member campuses to NSF 25-523 (EPSCoR E-CORE).

## Project Summary

SSI will build shared environmental sensing infrastructure spanning all four campuses
of the Sablecreek State University System, enabling coordinated research on working
lands — agricultural production, grazing systems and adjacent watersheds — and
strengthening the jurisdiction's research ecosystem.

## Results from Prior Support

Under NSF EPSCoR award SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021, "Statewide Research Infrastructure for
Precision Agriculture" ($3,200,000, 2021–2026), 3 of the 4 campuses participated, 47
faculty were engaged, 6 shared instruments were deployed and 22 graduate students were
supported. The award generated 5 follow-on external awards.

## Consortium Structure

The system office serves as lead applicant. Subawards will be issued to all four member
campuses: Harlow Bend, Grier Falls, Pellamy and Ondrak. All four campuses have committed
to participation and have provided letters of commitment.

Facilities and administrative costs are applied to the first $50,000 of each subaward.

## Research Plan

### Thrust 1 — Sensor network deployment

Sensor-network base stations and eddy covariance towers, with distributed soil-moisture
and weather nodes, will be deployed across representative working lands near each campus.

### Thrust 2 — Data integration

A shared data platform will ingest, quality-control and publish the observational
streams.

### Thrust 3 — Workforce and training

Graduate and undergraduate training will be coordinated across campuses.

## Letters

Letters of collaboration are included from the Harlow Bend and Grier Falls campuses, and
a letter of support from the Northern Plains Agricultural Commission.

## Institutional Cost Share

The system and its campuses have committed $250,000 in institutional cost share toward
this project, shown on the budget.

## Budget Summary

Total requested: $9,644,873 over four years. Fringe benefits are applied at the
negotiated 30.4% and indirect costs at 47.0% of modified total direct cost, including
the sensor-network base stations and towers.
