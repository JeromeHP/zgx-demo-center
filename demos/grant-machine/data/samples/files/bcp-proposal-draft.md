# Bridging Computing Pathways for Rural Transfer Students (BCP)

## Project Summary

Tierra Sandoval University proposes BCP to NSF S-STEM (NSF 25-514, Track 2), a six-year
scholarship programme for academically talented, low-income students transferring from
rural community colleges into computing degrees.

As a Hispanic-Serving Institution with 31% Hispanic enrollment, Tierra Sandoval is well
positioned to reach the students this programme serves.

**Intellectual Merit.** The project advances understanding of transfer student
persistence in computing disciplines.

**Broader Impacts.** The project serves first-generation and Pell-eligible students in a
region with limited access to computing careers.

## Scholarships

Each year 11 scholars will receive scholarships of $16,500, for $181,500 a year.

## Results from Prior Support

Under NSF award TSU-FICT-HSI-2022, "Pathways to Computing Degrees for Transfer
Students" ($449,800, 2022–2025), the project served 214 students and produced 61
transfer completions. Four new courses were developed and 320 hours of faculty
professional development were delivered. Two of the five planned articulation agreements
were signed.

## Project Description

### Objective 1 — Scholar cohorts

Scholars will join a cohort with faculty mentoring and a summer bridge experience.

### Objective 2 — Curriculum alignment

We will align lower-division computing curricula with partner community colleges to
guarantee transfer credit.

### Objective 3 — Advising and support

Dedicated transfer advising will be provided through the transfer centre.

## Evaluation Plan

The evaluation will be designed and conducted by an analyst in the university's Office
of Institutional Research, tracking retention, transfer completion and time to degree,
and reporting annually to the provost.

## Budget Summary

Total requested: $1,753,458 over six years. Fringe benefits are applied at the negotiated
29.6% and indirect costs at 41.5%, applied to all direct costs including the
scholarships.
