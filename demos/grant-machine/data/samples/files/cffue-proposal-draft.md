# Compound Flood Forecasting for Under-Instrumented Estuaries (CFFUE)

## Project Summary

The Calderwick Institute for Coastal Systems proposes CFFUE to the NSF Ocean Sciences
program (NSF 26-516), a three-year project to deliver operational compound flood
forecasts for microtidal estuaries that lack dense instrumentation. The work couples a
calibrated hydrodynamic model with low-cost sensor networks and a data assimilation
scheme, producing 48-hour forecasts at sub-kilometre resolution.

**Intellectual Merit.** The project advances data assimilation for sparse observational
networks, a persistent barrier to forecasting in under-instrumented systems.

**Broader Impacts.** Forecast products will be delivered directly to county emergency
managers serving vulnerable coastal populations, and the project trains a postdoctoral
researcher and a graduate student.

## Results from Prior Support

Under NSF award CICS-FICT-OCE-2021, "Sediment Transport Under Compound Flooding in
Microtidal Estuaries" ($842,600, 2021–2025), the team produced 9 peer-reviewed
publications and supported 4 graduate students. All 8 planned field campaigns were
executed successfully and 3 datasets were published to a public repository.

## Research Plan

### Objective 1 — Sensor network design

We will deploy an acoustic Doppler current profiler and six bottom-mounted pressure
loggers, selected by an optimal-placement analysis.

### Objective 2 — Model coupling and assimilation

The hydrodynamic model will be coupled to a wave module and an ensemble Kalman filter
assimilation scheme.

### Objective 3 — Operational delivery and evaluation

Forecast products will be delivered to partner agencies and evaluated for usability.
