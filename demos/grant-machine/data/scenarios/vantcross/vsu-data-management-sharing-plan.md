## Data Management and Sharing Plan

**Data Types and Standards**
The proposed project, *Mitochondrial Quality Control as a Determinant of Muscle Insulin Sensitivity* (MQC), will generate diverse datasets spanning molecular physiology, bioinformatics, and human clinical metrics. Primary data types include:
1.  **Human Clinical Data:** De-identified metabolic phenotyping results, blood biomarker concentrations, and participant demographic information.
2.  **Molecular and Omics Data:** Mitochondrial respiration rates, transcriptomic profiles, and proteomic datasets from skeletal muscle biopsies.
3.  **Animal Model Data:** Phenotypic measurements and histological images from murine models.

All datasets will adhere to FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) principles. Human-derived data will be coded to protect participant identity in compliance with HIPAA guidelines. Omics data will follow MIAME and MINSEQE standards. Clinical data will utilize CDISC standards where applicable. Metadata will be documented using standardized templates provided by the Vantcross State University Biostatistics Consulting Service.

**Repository Deposit**
Primary datasets will be deposited in NIH-approved repositories upon acceptance of manuscripts.
*   **Omics Data:** Raw and processed sequencing data will be deposited in the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) or the ProteomeXchange consortium.
*   **Clinical Data:** De-identified clinical data will be deposited in the NIH Data Archive (NDA) or a similar NIH-approved repository.
*   **Code and Protocols:** Analysis scripts and detailed protocols will be archived in GitHub (with a DOI via Zenodo) and the Vantcross State University institutional repository.

**Timeline for Sharing**
Data sharing will occur in phases aligned with the project’s five-year timeline (2027–2032):
*   **Year 1–2:** Development of data management protocols and IRB/IACUC approvals. Initial pilot data will be shared internally within the Vantcross research team and collaborators (Pellworth Metabolic Core, Drumlin Analytical Institute).
*   **Year 3–4:** As manuscripts are prepared for publication, corresponding datasets will be deposited in public repositories. A minimum of six months’ delay may be applied to protect intellectual property, consistent with NIH policy.
*   **Year 5:** Final datasets from the completed study will be fully archived and accessible. A data availability statement will be included in all resulting publications.

**Access Controls for Human-Derived Data**
Given the human-derived nature of the data, strict access controls are mandated. All human subject data will be de-identified prior to deposition. Controlled-access datasets will be submitted to the NDA, requiring researchers to submit a data use certificate and receive approval from the Institutional Review Board (IRB) at Vantcross State University. Access to raw, potentially identifiable data is restricted to the Principal Investigator (Dr. Rurik Anselmi) and designated co-investigators (Dr. Pernille Adeyemo-Strand) under the approved IRB protocol. No identifiable data will be shared publicly.

**Preservation Responsibilities**
Vantcross State University assumes long-term preservation responsibility for all data generated under this award. The University’s Office of Sponsored Programs, in coordination with the University Library’s digital repository services, will ensure data integrity and accessibility for a minimum of three years post-award. Dr. Corwin Blathwaite, Executive Director of Sponsored Programs, will oversee compliance with data retention policies. In the event of personnel changes, data stewardship will be transferred to a designated successor or the department chair. The University guarantees that all data will remain accessible via persistent identifiers (DOIs) and will not be removed from public repositories without valid legal or ethical justification.

**Collaborator Roles**
*   **Pellworth Metabolic Core:** Will provide technical support for metabolic phenotyping data collection and ensure data standardization.
*   **Drumlin Analytical Institute:** Will process and analyze metabolomics and lipidomics data, adhering to Vantcross’s data management protocols.

**Compliance**
This plan complies with NIH Policy for Data Management and Sharing (NOT-OD-21-013). Any deviations from this plan will be reported to the NIH Division of Program Officers and the Vantcross State University Office of Research Compliance.
