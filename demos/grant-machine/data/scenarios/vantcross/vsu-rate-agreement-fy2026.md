## Negotiated Indirect Cost and Fringe Benefit Rate Agreement FY2026

**Institution:** Vantcross State University (Vantcross)  
**Effective Period:** October 1, 2025 – September 30, 2026  
**Agreement Number:** VSU-NICR-FY26-001  

### 1. Purpose and Scope

This Negotiated Indirect Cost Rate Agreement (hereinafter "the Agreement") establishes the rates at which Vantcross State University (Vantcross) will charge Federal, State, and local government agencies, as well as private sponsors, for indirect costs associated with sponsored research projects. This document serves as the authoritative reference for budget preparation, compliance review, and financial administration for all grants and cooperative agreements active during the fiscal year 2026.

All Principal Investigators (PIs), Co-Principal Investigators (Co-PIs), and administrative staff responsible for grant budgeting must adhere strictly to the rates defined herein. Deviations from these negotiated rates without prior written approval from the Office of Sponsored Programs are prohibited.

### 2. Negotiated Rates

The following rates have been negotiated between Vantcross State University and its cognizant federal agency for indirect costs and fringe benefits. These rates apply to all on-campus research activities unless otherwise specified below.

#### 2.1 Fringe Benefit Rate
The negotiated fringe benefit rate for FY2026 is **33.8%**.

This rate applies to the salaries and wages of employees engaged in sponsored projects, including faculty, staff, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows. The rate is calculated based on Vantcross’s historical fringe benefit costs, including health insurance, retirement contributions, payroll taxes, and other mandatory benefits, divided by total adjusted salary base.

#### 2.2 Indirect Cost Rate (Facilities and Administrative Costs)
The negotiated indirect cost rate for on-campus organized research is **58.0%** of Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC).

Indirect costs are defined as those costs incurred for a common or joint purpose benefiting more than one cost objective, which cannot be readily identified with a specific cost objective. The base for this calculation is MTDC, which includes all salaries and wages, fringe benefits, materials and supplies, services, travel, and up to the first $25,000 of each subaward (or subcontract) regardless of the period covered by the subaward.

### 3. Subaward Indirect Cost Policy

Vantcross State University adheres to federal regulations regarding the application of indirect costs to subawards. The policy is as follows:

*   **First $25,000:** Indirect costs will be applied only to the first $25,000 of each subaward’s budget period.
*   **Amounts Exceeding $25,000:** No indirect costs are charged on the portion of the subaward that exceeds $25,000 in any given budget period.

This policy ensures compliance with Uniform Guidance (2 CFR § 200.332) and prevents double-charging for administrative support already provided by the subrecipient. Budgets must reflect this limitation explicitly. Any attempt to apply the full institutional indirect cost rate to the entire subaward amount is non-compliant and will result in budget disallowance during financial review.

### 4. Administrative Provisions

#### 4.1 Rate Application
These rates are fixed for the twelve-month period specified above. They are not subject to adjustment based on actual expenditures unless a formal renegotiation process is initiated and approved by the cognizant agency prior to the end of the fiscal year.

#### 4.2 Compliance and Audit
Vantcross State University certifies that these rates were developed in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles and applicable federal cost principles. The Office of Sponsored Programs, led by Executive Director Corwin Blathwaite, is responsible for monitoring the application of these rates across all active awards. Regular audits will be conducted to ensure that budgets align with this agreement and that no unauthorized charges are made to sponsored accounts.
