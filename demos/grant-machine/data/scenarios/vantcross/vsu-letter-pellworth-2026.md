## Letter of Collaboration

**To:** Dr. Rurik Anselmi, Professor of Molecular Physiology, Vantcross State University
**From:** Dr. Elias Thorne, Director, Pellworth Metabolic Core
**Date:** August 14, 2026
**Re:** Collaboration Support for Project MQC (Mitochondrial Quality Control as a Determinant of Muscle Insulin Sensitivity)

This letter confirms the active collaboration between the Pellworth Metabolic Core and the research team led by Dr. Rurik Anselmi for the proposed five-year project, *Mitochondrial Quality Control as a Determinant of Muscle Insulin Sensitivity* (MQC), commencing July 1, 2027.

The Pellworth Metabolic Core guarantees Dr. Anselmi and Co-Investigator Dr. Pernille Adeyemo-Strand dedicated access to our specialized metabolic phenotyping instrumentation. This includes the high-resolution extracellular flux analyzer suite and the automated calorimetry array, which are critical for the project’s primary aims. We have reserved specific instrument blocks to ensure uninterrupted data acquisition for your mouse colony studies and human cohort biopsies.

Technical support will be provided by our senior staff, including Dr. Aris Vane and Lab Technician Sarah Lin. They will assist with assay optimization, data normalization, and the interpretation of complex metabolic flux profiles. This support is essential for maintaining the rigor of the phenotyping data and ensuring that the metabolic readouts align with the bioinformatics integration performed by Dr. Adeyemo-Strand.

The Core will also facilitate the integration of metabolic data with the genomic outputs from the Drumlin Analytical Institute subaward. Our facility is fully equipped to handle the volume and sensitivity of samples generated by the MQC project, and we have coordinated with your team to streamline sample transport and processing protocols.

We are committed to supporting the scientific goals of the MQC project and look forward to a successful collaboration over the next five years.

Sincerely,

Dr. Elias Thorne
Director
Pellworth Metabolic Core
Vantcross State University
