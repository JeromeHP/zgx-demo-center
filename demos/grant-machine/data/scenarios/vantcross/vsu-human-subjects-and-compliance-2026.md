## Regulatory Approvals and Protocol Status

This section outlines the current regulatory compliance status for the proposed project, *Mitochondrial Quality Control as a Determinant of Muscle Insulin Sensitivity* (MQC). All human subjects and vertebrate animal activities are governed by Vantcross State University’s Institutional Review Board (IRB) and Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC), respectively.

### Human Subjects Research

The MQC project involves the recruitment of a human cohort to assess insulin sensitivity via hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamps. This activity is covered under IRB Protocol #2025-HS-449. The protocol was granted expedited review status due to the minimal risk nature of the procedures involved.

*   **Approval Date:** January 15, 2026
*   **Expiry Date:** January 14, 2027

The principal investigator, Dr. Rurik Anselmi, has confirmed that all research staff listed on this protocol have completed required CITI training in human subjects protection prior to engaging in any study-related activities. The IRB continues to monitor this protocol for ongoing compliance with the Declaration of Helsinki and 45 CFR 46.

### Vertebrate Animal Research

The project also requires the use of C57BL/6J mice to evaluate mitochondrial dynamics in skeletal muscle tissue. This work is authorized under IACUC Protocol #2025-VA-112. The protocol adheres strictly to the Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals (8th Edition) and USDA regulations.

*   **Approval Date:** March 10, 2026
*   **Expiry Date:** March 9, 2027

The IACUC has reviewed the justification for animal use, confirming that non-animal alternatives were considered but deemed insufficient for the physiological measurements required. Veterinary oversight is provided by the Vantcross Office of Laboratory Animal Resources, with routine health monitoring and husbandry protocols in place. Any adverse events will be reported to the IACUC within 24 hours as per institutional policy.

## Biosafety and Conflict of Interest

### Biosafety Registration

All laboratory work involving biological agents is conducted under BSL-2 conditions. The project is registered with the Vantcross Environmental Health and Safety Office under BioSafety Registration #VSU-BIO-2026-88. The Principal Investigator has submitted a completed Biological Safety Committee (BSC) application detailing the use of recombinant DNA constructs and viral vectors for mitochondrial transfer experiments. All personnel have completed annual biosafety training, including proper handling of infectious materials and emergency response procedures.
