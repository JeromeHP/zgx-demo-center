## Strategic Vision and Institutional Context

Vantcross State University, a public R1 doctoral university with very high research activity, serves the Upper Midwest through a comprehensive portfolio of teaching, research, and public service. With eleven colleges and an annual research expenditure of $412 million, Vantcross has established itself as a critical engine for scientific discovery and economic development in the region. This University Research Strategic Plan 2026-2031 outlines the institution’s commitment to advancing knowledge through five priority areas while strengthening the foundational infrastructure required for sustained excellence.

The university supports a robust research ecosystem comprising 1,840 research staff and 6,200 graduate students. These individuals operate within a framework governed by the Office of Sponsored Programs, which ensures rigorous compliance with federal regulations and ethical standards. As we enter this new strategic period, Vantcross aims to deepen interdisciplinary collaboration, enhance training opportunities for the next generation of scientists, and address complex societal challenges through targeted investment in emerging fields.

## Five-Year Research Priorities

The 2026-2031 strategic plan identifies five interconnected domains where Vantcross will concentrate its resources and intellectual capital. These priorities reflect both the institution’s historical strengths and the evolving needs of the scientific community and the public it serves.

**Metabolic and Cardiovascular Disease**
Vantcross continues to build upon its legacy in molecular physiology and metabolic disorders. Recent successes, including significant NIH-funded investigations into mitochondrial dynamics and skeletal muscle insulin resistance, demonstrate our capacity for high-impact translational research. The university will expand efforts to understand the molecular mechanisms underlying cardiovascular disease, leveraging core facilities for metabolic phenotyping and genomics to accelerate discoveries from bench to bedside.

**Computational Biology and Data Science**
Recognizing the transformative power of data-driven science, Vantcross is investing heavily in computational biology. This priority area focuses on integrating multi-omics data with clinical outcomes to identify novel therapeutic targets. By enhancing our high-performance computing infrastructure and fostering collaborations between biologists and data scientists, we aim to develop new analytical frameworks that can handle the complexity of biological systems.

**Materials for Energy Storage**
Addressing the global energy transition is a key component of our strategic agenda. Vantcross will focus on the design and synthesis of advanced materials for next-generation energy storage devices. This work involves close collaboration between chemistry, physics, and engineering departments to create sustainable solutions for battery technology and renewable energy integration.

**Agricultural Systems**
As a land-grant institution with deep roots in the Upper Midwest, Vantcross remains dedicated to improving agricultural productivity and sustainability. Research in this area will concentrate on crop resilience, soil health, and efficient resource use. By applying cutting-edge genetic tools and environmental modeling, we seek to support farmers in adapting to climate variability while minimizing ecological impact.

**Research Infrastructure Renewal**
Sustained excellence requires robust physical and digital infrastructure. This strategic plan allocates significant resources toward the renewal of aging laboratory spaces and the modernization of shared instrumentation. Key upgrades will include cryo-electron microscopy capabilities, expanded biostatistics consulting services, and enhanced data management systems. These improvements are essential for maintaining our status as a competitive research environment.

## Graduate Training Capacity

The vitality of Vantcross’s research enterprise is inextricably linked to the quality and quantity of its graduate training programs. With 6,200 graduate students currently enrolled, the university provides extensive opportunities for hands-on research experience under the mentorship of world-class faculty.

Our strategic approach to graduate education emphasizes interdisciplinary training, ensuring that students are equipped with diverse skill sets relevant to both academic and non-academic career paths. We are expanding fellowships and professional development workshops focused on data management, scientific communication, and leadership. Furthermore, we are strengthening partnerships with industry and government agencies to create internship opportunities that bridge the gap between academia and the workforce. By investing in our students, Vantcross ensures a steady pipeline of talented researchers who will continue to drive innovation and address global challenges in the decades to come.
