## Position

Dr. Rurik Anselmi is Professor of Molecular Physiology at Vantcross State University, a public R1 doctoral university in the Upper Midwest. Dr. Anselmi leads the Muscle Metabolism Laboratory within the Department of Physiology, where he directs a team of postdoctoral fellows, research technicians, and graduate students. His laboratory investigates the molecular mechanisms linking mitochondrial dynamics to metabolic disease, with a specific focus on skeletal muscle insulin resistance.

## Training and Research Focus

Dr. Anselmi received his Ph.D. in Cell and Molecular Biology from the University of Chicago in 2012, followed by postdoctoral training in metabolic physiology at the Joslin Diabetes Center. His research program integrates in vivo mouse models, primary myocyte culture, and multi-omics approaches to dissect mitochondrial quality control pathways. He is particularly interested in how mitophagy and mitochondrial biogenesis regulate insulin signaling cascades in aging and obese states.

Dr. Anselmi’s work has been supported by continuous funding from the National Institutes of Health since 2016. His current research aims to define the causal relationship between mitochondrial fission/fusion balance and glucose uptake efficiency. He serves on the editorial board of *Metabolism* and is a member of the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology.
