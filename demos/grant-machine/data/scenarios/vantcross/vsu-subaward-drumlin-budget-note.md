## Subaward Scope and Period of Performance

The subaward to Drumlin Analytical Institute supports the targeted metabolomics and lipidomics analysis of skeletal muscle biopsy specimens collected under the proposed project, *Mitochondrial Quality Control as a Determinant of Muscle Insulin Sensitivity*. Drumlin Analytical Institute will perform comprehensive profiling of acylcarnitines, free fatty acids, and phospholipid species using high-resolution mass spectrometry. The scope of work includes sample preparation, chromatographic separation, data acquisition, and initial bioinformatic normalization of lipidomic datasets. The period of performance for this subaward aligns with the five-year duration of the prime award, commencing on July 1, 2027, and concluding on June 30, 2032.

## Budget Details

The total cost for the subaward is $375,000 over the five-year period. The annual budget is fixed at $75,000 per year. This amount covers the direct costs of analytical services, including instrument time, specialized reagents, and personnel labor required to process the anticipated volume of muscle biopsy samples. The breakdown of the annual cost is as follows:

*   **Year 1 (2027–2028):** $75,000
*   **Year 2 (2028–2029):** $75,000
*   **Year 3 (2029–2030):** $75,000
*   **Year 4 (2030–2031):** $75,000
*   **Year 5 (2031–2032):** $75,000
*   **Total:** $375,000

## Facilities and Administrative Costs

Vantcross State University applies its negotiated indirect cost rate to the subaward budget in accordance with its current rate agreement. As routine practice, the prime applies its 58.0% F&A rate to the full $75,000 each year, resulting in an annual F&A figure of $43,500.
