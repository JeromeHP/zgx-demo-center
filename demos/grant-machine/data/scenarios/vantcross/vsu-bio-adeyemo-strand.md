## Current Position

Dr. Pernille Adeyemo-Strand serves as Associate Professor of Bioinformatics in the Department of Computational Biology at Vantcross State University (Vantcross). She joined the faculty in 2018, having previously held a postdoctoral fellowship in statistical genomics at the Institute for Data Science. Her academic training includes a Ph.D. in Biostatistics from Oakhaven University and a B.S. in Computer Science from Vantcross State University.

## Research Focus

Dr. Adeyemo-Strand’s research program focuses on the development and application of computational methods for multi-omics integration, with specific emphasis on mitochondrial dynamics and metabolic regulation. Her work bridges high-dimensional data analysis with biological interpretation, aiming to identify molecular signatures of insulin resistance in skeletal muscle. She leads the bioinformatics core for the university’s Metabolic Disease Initiative, overseeing the analytical pipeline for genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics datasets. Her expertise lies in developing robust statistical frameworks that account for batch effects and confounding variables in large-scale biological studies, ensuring reproducibility and interpretability of multi-omic findings.
