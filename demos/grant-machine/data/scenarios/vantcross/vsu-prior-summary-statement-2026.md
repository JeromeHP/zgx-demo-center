## Summary Statement — Prior Submission (for resubmission reference)

**Application Number:** 1R01DK123456-01
**Title:** Mitochondrial Dynamics in Skeletal Muscle Insulin Resistance
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Rurik Anselmi, Vantcross State University
**Date of Summary Statement:** September 12, 2026
**Impact Score:** 28 (Highly Competitive)

### Overall Impression

The review panel commends Dr. Anselmi and the research team for their rigorous approach to investigating the role of mitochondrial dynamics in skeletal muscle insulin resistance. The project addresses a significant gap in our understanding of metabolic disease pathophysiology. The scientific premise is sound, and the preliminary data generated during the prior award period demonstrate a strong foundation for the proposed studies. However, the panel identified several critical weaknesses that preclude funding at this time. Specifically, concerns regarding the sufficiency of preliminary data for the third aim, the Principal Investigator’s available effort, the clarity of the statistical analysis plan, and the defined scientific role of the subaward partner must be addressed in any resubmission.

### Specific Critiques

**1. Insufficient Preliminary Data for Aim 3**
While Aims 1 and 2 are well-supported by robust preliminary data, the panel found the justification for Aim 3—investigating the specific contribution of mitophagy flux to insulin signaling—to be speculative. The current submission relies heavily on published literature rather than original data generated by the applicant. The panel noted that the prior award successfully completed only two of its three proposed aims, with the third aim remaining incomplete due to technical delays. Without new, applicant-generated preliminary data demonstrating the feasibility of the proposed mitophagy assays in the specific muscle fiber types under investigation, the risk associated with Aim 3 is deemed too high. The resubmission must include preliminary data from the applicant’s laboratory that directly validates the key experimental strategies proposed in this aim.

**2. Concerns Regarding Principal Investigator Effort**
The panel has significant concerns regarding the Principal Investigator’s ability to dedicate sufficient time to this project. The current-and-pending support documentation indicates that Dr. Anselmi has committed effort across multiple active grants and pending applications that total more than 100% of his professional time. While the PI is a distinguished scientist, the panel questions whether the proposed 30% effort is realistic given the competing demands of his current commitments. There is a risk that the project will suffer from inadequate oversight and mentorship if the PI’s attention is fragmented. The resubmission must provide a clear and realistic justification for the PI’s available time, potentially including a revised current-and-pending support list that reflects the current status of all active and pending commitments.

**3. Underspecified Statistical Analysis Plan**
The statistical methodology outlined in the application is overly generic. The panel found that the description of sample size calculations, power analysis, and specific statistical tests for each aim lacks the necessary detail to ensure rigorous data interpretation. For example, the plan for handling missing data in the human cohort component and the specific models for longitudinal muscle biopsy analysis are not sufficiently described. The panel recommends that the resubmission include a detailed statistical analysis plan, ideally developed in consultation with a biostatistician, that explicitly addresses the primary and secondary endpoints, the handling of covariates, and the criteria for interim analysis if applicable.

**4. Unclear Scientific Role of Subaward**
The role of the subaward partner, Drumlin Analytical Institute, was not clearly defined in terms of its scientific contribution to the project. While the letter of collaboration confirms the availability of analytical services, the application does adequately explain how the specific metabolomic and lipidomic data generated by Drumlin will be integrated into the overarching hypothesis of the project. It is unclear whether Drumlin is providing a specialized service that is uniquely necessary or if the work could be performed in-house or through other available resources. The resubmission must clarify the scientific necessity of this subaward and detail the specific deliverables and intellectual contribution expected from the partner institution.

### Conclusion

The project is scientifically important and the team is well-qualified. However, the concerns regarding preliminary data for Aim 3, PI effort, statistical rigor, and the subaward’s scientific role are significant. The panel encourages the applicant to address these points thoroughly in a resubmission.
