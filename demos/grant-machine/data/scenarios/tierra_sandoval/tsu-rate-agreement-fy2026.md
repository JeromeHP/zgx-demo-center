## Agreement Overview and Authority

This Negotiated Indirect Cost and Fringe Benefit Rate Agreement (the "Agreement") establishes the allowable rates for indirect costs and fringe benefits applicable to Tierra Sandoval University (Tierra Sandoval) for Fiscal Year 2026. This document serves as the authoritative benchmark against which all budget submissions, including those for the proposed project *Bridging Computing Pathways for Rural Transfer Students* (BCP), are verified and audited. Tierra Sandoval, a public regional comprehensive university designated as a Hispanic-Serving Institution, operates under the administrative oversight of its Office of Grants and Contracts. The rates detailed herein have been negotiated in accordance with federal guidelines and institutional policy, ensuring compliance for all sponsored projects initiated during this fiscal period.

## Fringe Benefit Rates

The fringe benefit rate applicable to all direct costs associated with personnel salaries and wages is established at **29.6%**. This percentage reflects the university’s comprehensive calculation of mandatory and discretionary benefits, including but not limited to social security contributions, Medicare, health insurance premiums, retirement plan contributions, and paid leave accruals.

For the current fiscal year, this rate is applied uniformly across all employee classifications involved in sponsored projects. The basis for this calculation includes historical data from payroll records and projected benefit cost increases approved by the university’s Board of Trustees. Any deviation from this rate requires explicit written approval from the Director of Grants and Contracts, Marisela Thorncroft, and must be justified by significant changes in institutional benefits structures or federal regulatory requirements.

## Indirect Cost Rates

Tierra Sandoval has negotiated an on-campus indirect cost rate of **41.5%** applied to Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC). This rate is designed to recover a fair share of the institution’s general and administrative expenses that are not directly attributable to a specific project but are necessary for its conduct. These costs include facility maintenance, library resources, information technology infrastructure, human resources administration, and other central services.

The base for this calculation is Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC), which includes salaries and wages, fringe benefits, materials and supplies, services, travel, and subawards up to the first $25,000 of each subaward. MTDC excludes equipment, capital expenditures, charges for patient care, rental costs, tuition remission, scholarships, fellowships, and subawards in excess of $25,000. This structure ensures that indirect cost recovery is proportional to the actual resources consumed by the project without penalizing high-equipment or high-subaward activities.
