## Biographical Sketch

**Position**
Dr. Nayeli Arbuthnot serves as the Associate Provost for Student Success at Tierra Sandoval University (Tierra Sandoval), a public regional comprehensive university and designated Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) located in the inland Southwest. In this capacity, Dr. Arbuthnot oversees institutional strategies aimed at improving retention, completion, and equity outcomes for underrepresented student populations. With a total enrollment of 9,420 students—comprising 63% first-generation college students, 58% Pell Grant recipients, and 26.4% Hispanic students—Tierra Sandoval operates in a service area with significant educational access challenges. Dr. Arbuthnot reports directly to the President, Dr. Esperanza Quintanilla-Boyd, and collaborates closely with the Director of Grants and Contracts, Marisela Thorncroft, to ensure fiscal and programmatic compliance across all sponsored projects.

**Background in Student Success Research**
Dr. Arbuthnot’s academic career is defined by a rigorous focus on transfer student pathways and STEM equity. Her research examines structural barriers faced by community college transfers entering four-year institutions, particularly within technology disciplines. This expertise directly informed the leadership of Tierra Sandoval’s prior NSF-funded initiative, *Pathways to Computing Degrees for Transfer Students* (Award ID: TSU-FICT-HSI-2022), which ran from September 1, 2022, to August 31, 2025. Under her direction, this project served 214 students and achieved 61 transfer completions in computing-related fields. Dr. Arbuthnot also led the development of four new curriculum modules and facilitated 320 hours of faculty professional development focused on inclusive pedagogies. Furthermore, her administrative work resulted in the signing of two articulation agreements with local community colleges, with five additional agreements currently in planning stages. Her ability to translate research into actionable institutional policy has made her a pivotal figure in Tierra Sandoval’s HSI designation confirmation for the current cycle.

**Selected Publications and Project Leadership**
Dr. Arbuthnot has authored numerous peer-reviewed articles on transfer student persistence and workforce alignment in rural contexts. She serves as the Principal Investigator (PI) for the proposed project, *Bridging Computing Pathways for Rural Transfer Students* (BCP), submitted under NSF Solicitation 25-514 (S-STEM Program, Track 2). This six-year implementation project, commencing August 1, 2027, aims to support 11 scholars annually. Dr. Arbuthnot leads a multidisciplinary team that includes Dr. Elias Vorderman, Chair of the Department of Computer Information Systems, who serves as Co-PI. Together, they have secured strong letters of support from key partners, including Cordillera Basin Community College (dated August 11, 2026) and the Saltbush Regional Workforce Board (dated August 19, 2026), underscoring the regional ecosystem’s commitment to the project’s success.
