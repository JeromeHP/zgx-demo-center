## Prior Award Context

The CFFUE proposal builds upon the successful completion of award CICS-FICT-OCE-2021 (*Sediment Transport Under Compound Flooding in Microtidal Estuaries*, 2021–2025). That prior effort, funded by the NSF Division of Ocean Sciences with $842,600, resulted in nine peer-reviewed papers, support for four graduate students, three published datasets, and the completion of five field campaigns. The current submission leverages these outcomes to expand forecasting capabilities into under-instrumented regions.
