## Agreement Authority and Scope

This Negotiated Indirect Cost and Fringe Benefit Rate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the Calderwick Institute for Coastal Systems (CICS), a research institute within Alderbrook State University, and the United States government acting through its cognizant federal agency. This document establishes the allowable rates and methodologies for calculating indirect costs and fringe benefits for all federally sponsored awards administered by CICS during Fiscal Year 2026.

As a specialized entity focused on Mid-Atlantic estuarine and nearshore systems, CICS operates under the administrative umbrella of Alderbrook State University. The Institute currently supports a research staff of 41 individuals and engages 28 graduate students in its core missions. With an annual research expenditure totaling $9,400,000, CICS maintains rigorous financial compliance standards to ensure that federal funds are utilized efficiently and transparently. This Agreement serves as the definitive benchmark against which all budget submissions, including those for the upcoming "Compound Flood Forecasting for Under-Instrumented Estuaries" (CFFUE) project, must be checked.

## Fringe Benefit Rates

The fringe benefit rate specified in this Agreement is set at 31.5% of total salaries and wages. This rate encompasses all direct charges for employee benefits, including but not limited to retirement contributions, health insurance, social security taxes, and other legally mandated or institutionally provided benefits. The calculation base for this rate is strictly limited to direct salary charges incurred on the sponsored project.

The application of this 31.5% rate ensures that the cost of maintaining a skilled workforce—critical to CICS’s operational capacity—is fully recovered without imposing an undue burden on the federal sponsor. This rate is derived from comprehensive institutional financial audits and has been negotiated in accordance with federal guidelines. It applies uniformly to all personnel, including principal investigators, co-principal investigators, research scientists, and support staff, regardless of their appointment type or funding source, provided they are directly charged to the award.

## Indirect Cost Rates

The on-campus research indirect cost rate is established at 54.5% of Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC). MTDC includes all direct salaries and wages, fringe benefits, materials, supplies, services, travel, and subawards up to the first $25,000 of each subaward. Excluded from MTDC are capital expenditures, equipment costs, tuition waivers, and participant support costs, in compliance with federal cost principles.

This 54.5% rate reflects the substantial institutional overhead required to support CICS’s research infrastructure, including laboratory maintenance, administrative support, library resources, and utilities. As a unit within Alderbrook State University, CICS leverages shared campus facilities while maintaining distinct operational needs for coastal fieldwork and data processing. The negotiated rate ensures that the Institute can sustain its high-caliber research environment, supporting ongoing projects such as the prior award CICS-FICT-OCE-2021 and future initiatives like CFFUE.

## Application and Compliance

This Agreement is effective for Fiscal Year 2026 and supersedes any previous rate agreements that may have been in effect during this period. All budget proposals submitted by CICS, including the draft budget for the CFFUE project (document ID: cics-budget-cffue-draft), must adhere strictly to these rates. Any deviation from the 31.5% fringe benefit rate or the 54.5% indirect cost rate requires prior written approval from the cognizant federal agency.

The Institute’s Director of Research Administration, Beatrix Okonjo-Hale, is responsible for ensuring that all financial documentation aligns with this Agreement. In the context of the CFFUE project, which spans three years starting July 1, 2027, and is associated with solicitation NSF 26-516, these rates will be applied to the requested total of $1,029,198. This ensures that the financial framework for future expenditures remains consistent with current negotiated terms, providing stability for both the Institute and its federal partners.
