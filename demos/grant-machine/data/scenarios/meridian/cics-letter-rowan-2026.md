## Purpose and Scope of Collaboration

This letter serves to formally document the collaborative partnership between the Calderwick Institute for Coastal Systems (CICS) and the Rowan Tidal Observatory (RTO) regarding the proposed project, *Compound Flood Forecasting for Under-Instrumented Estuaries* (CFFUE). As CICS prepares its submission under NSF Solicitation 26-516, this agreement outlines the specific contributions RTO will make to enhance the project’s data integrity and spatial coverage. The collaboration is designed to leverage RTO’s established infrastructure to complement CICS’s modeling capabilities, specifically addressing the need for high-fidelity tidal records in microtidal estuarine environments.

## Shared Access to Tide Gauge Records

A cornerstone of this partnership is the guaranteed shared access to historical and real-time tide gauge records maintained by the Rowan Tidal Observatory. RTO will provide CICS with unrestricted access to its long-term water level datasets, which are critical for calibrating the compound flood models proposed in the CFFUE track. These records will serve as ground-truth validation for the atmospheric and oceanic forcing inputs generated by CICS’s Coastal Process Modelling team. By integrating RTO’s precise tidal datums with CICS’s sediment dynamics simulations, the project aims to reduce uncertainty in flood forecasting for under-instrumented regions within the Mid-Atlantic estuarine service area.

## Co-Located Deployments and Field Operations

In addition to data sharing, the collaboration includes plans for co-located instrument deployments during the proposed three-year project period (starting 2027-07-01). RTO will facilitate access to its existing sensor platforms and logistical support for joint field campaigns. This co-location strategy allows for simultaneous measurement of hydrodynamic forces and sediment transport processes, directly supporting the project’s objective to improve forecasting accuracy in complex coastal zones. The synergy between RTO’s observational expertise and CICS’s computational resources ensures a robust multi-disciplinary approach to understanding compound flooding mechanisms.

## Institutional Commitment and Signatories

The Calderwick Institute for Coastal Systems affirms its commitment to this collaboration, recognizing the Rowan Tidal Observatory as an essential partner in achieving the scientific goals of CFFUE. This partnership builds upon the successful outcomes of prior joint efforts, including the previous NSF-funded project *Sediment Transport Under Compound Flooding in Microtidal Estuaries* (CICS-FICT-OCE-2021), which resulted in nine peer-reviewed papers and five completed field campaigns. The integration of RTO’s resources will further strengthen the technical merit of this proposal, ensuring comprehensive data coverage and enhanced model validation.

We confirm that both institutions have reviewed the terms of this collaboration and agree to the shared responsibilities outlined herein. This letter is effective as of 2026-08-05 and remains valid throughout the duration of the proposed CFFUE project.
