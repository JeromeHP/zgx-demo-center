## Human Subjects and Animal Care Protocols

CICS operates under the oversight of both the Institutional Review Board (IRB) and the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC). For the proposed CFFUE initiative, the scope of human subjects involvement is minimal and strictly defined. The project focuses primarily on physical oceanographic modeling and sediment dynamics; however, limited engagement with community stakeholders in Quillon County for emergency management coordination necessitates IRB review. Dr. Imogen Sarkissian, Associate Director of Coastal Process Modelling, has confirmed that all data collection involving human participants will be anonymized and aggregated to ensure no identifiable private information is retained. The IRB has granted expedited review status for this limited engagement, ensuring that ethical standards are met without imposing undue administrative burden on the research timeline.

Similarly, the IACUC oversight is not applicable to CFFUE, as the project does not involve vertebrate animals in its methodology. This aligns with CICS’s broader compliance posture, where animal use is strictly confined to specific marine biology sub-projects under separate protocols, thereby isolating risk and ensuring clear regulatory boundaries for all personnel.
