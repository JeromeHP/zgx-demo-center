## Biographical Sketch: Dr. Imogen Sarkissian

**Position:** Associate Director, Coastal Process Modelling at the Calderwick Institute for Coastal Systems (CICS).

**Education and Training**
Dr. Imogen Sarkissian holds a Ph.D. in Ocean Engineering from Alderbrook State University, where her doctoral research focused on high-resolution hydrodynamic modeling of microtidal estuaries. Her postdoctoral training was conducted at the Rowan Tidal Observatory, specializing in data assimilation techniques for under-instrumented coastal zones. This interdisciplinary background bridges theoretical fluid dynamics with practical observational constraints, positioning her to address critical gaps in coastal resilience forecasting.

**Research Focus**
Dr. Sarkissian’s research program centers on advancing process-based models for compound flooding events in Mid-Atlantic estuarine systems. Her work integrates atmospheric forcing data with tidal hydrodynamics to improve predictive accuracy in regions lacking dense sensor networks. By coupling sediment transport modules with surge models, she seeks to quantify the synergistic effects of sea-level rise and extreme weather on coastal infrastructure. Dr. Sarkissian currently leads the Coastal Process Modelling group at CICS, overseeing a team of 12 research scientists and coordinating with 28 graduate students on active field campaigns.
