## Purpose and Scope

This Negotiated Indirect Cost and Fringe Benefit Rate Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the allowable rates for fringe benefits and on-campus organized research indirect costs applicable to all federally sponsored awards initiated by Ashgrove University Medical Center (Ashgrove) during Fiscal Year 2026. This document serves as the definitive reference for budget preparation, review, and compliance within the Office of Clinical Research Administration. All Principal Investigators, Co-Principal Investigators, and departmental administrators must utilize these negotiated rates when developing financial proposals to ensure consistency with institutional financial policies and federal regulations.

## Fringe Benefit Rates

The fringe benefit rate represents the estimated cost of employee benefits associated with direct salaries and wages. For FY2026, Ashgrove has established a uniform fringe benefit rate of **31.2%** applied to all modified total direct costs (MTDC) attributable to salary charges. This rate encompasses contributions for social security, Medicare, health insurance, retirement plans, and other mandated or voluntary employee benefits as defined by the institution’s human resources policies.

The Office of Clinical Research Administration, led by Director Lenore Kasprzak, certifies that this rate is derived from a comprehensive analysis of historical expenditure data and projected future liabilities. It is critical that all budget justifications accurately reflect this percentage to avoid underfunding of essential personnel support structures. Deviations from this standard rate require prior written approval from the Chief Research Officer, Dr. Yusuf Brandolini, and are subject to external sponsor review.

## Indirect Cost Rates for Organized Research

Indirect costs, also referred to as Facilities and Administrative (F&A) costs, are incurred for purposes common to both sponsored and other activities of Ashgrove and cannot be identified readily with a specific cost objective. For FY2026, the negotiated on-campus organized research indirect cost rate is set at **62.5%** of Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC).

This rate reflects the proportionate share of institutional overhead required to support the infrastructure necessary for conducting high-quality research. It includes costs such as library resources, utilities, building maintenance, and administrative support services. The application of this rate ensures that Ashgrove maintains the operational capacity to support its active clinical trials portfolio, which currently stands at 412 concurrent studies, and its annual research expenditure base of $268,000,000.

## Application and Compliance

These rates are effective for all grant proposals submitted on or after July 1, 2026, and through June 30, 2027. They apply to the new project "Stepped Mobilization and Sedation Minimization in Critical Illness" (SMSM), as well as any other awards managed under the current fiscal framework. Budgets prepared for submission to agencies such as the NIH must strictly adhere to these percentages. For instance, the draft budget for the SMSM track 2 proposal, totaling $3,706,990 over five years, has been validated against these specific rates to ensure financial integrity.

It is the responsibility of each Principal Investigator, including Dr. Saoirse Achterberg and Dr. Vikram Ostrowski-Baird for relevant ongoing and future initiatives, to verify that their budgets align with this Agreement. The Office of Clinical Research Administration will reject any budget documents that utilize outdated or unapproved rates. Furthermore, collaboration letters from partner institutions, such as Wrenfield Regional Hospital and Calloway Valley Health, must not assume indirect cost coverage unless explicitly stated in the primary award’s budget justification.
