## Commitment to Multi-Site Collaboration

Calloway Valley Health formally acknowledges its role as a participating site in the proposed research initiative titled *Stepped Mobilization and Sedation Minimization in Critical Illness* (SMSM). This letter serves to confirm our institutional commitment to executing the protocol with the same rigor and clinical precision demonstrated by our primary collaborator, Ashgrove University Medical Center. As an academic medical center within a four-hospital system serving the Mid-Atlantic region, we recognize the critical importance of standardized data collection and uniform patient management across diverse geographic sites.

## Intensive Care Capacity and Infrastructure

Our institution possesses substantial capacity to support the recruitment and monitoring requirements outlined in the SMSM protocol. With a total bed count of 1,140 and a dedicated faculty of 980 physicians, Calloway Valley Health maintains a robust infrastructure for critical care delivery. Our intensive care units are equipped with advanced hemodynamic monitoring systems and respiratory support technologies necessary to implement the stepped mobilization protocols without compromising patient safety.

The site’s existing engagement in 412 active clinical trials demonstrates our operational maturity in managing complex multi-center studies. We have established dedicated research nursing staff and pharmacy coordination teams specifically trained in sedation minimization techniques, ensuring that the intervention arms of this trial can be executed with high fidelity. Our facility’s annual research expenditure of $268,000,000 underscores our long-term dedication to advancing critical care medicine through rigorous scientific inquiry.

## Protocol Adherence and Oversight

Calloway Valley Health agrees to adhere strictly to the SMSM protocol, including all safety monitoring procedures and data reporting timelines. We will utilize the same electronic data capture systems as Ashgrove University Medical Center to ensure interoperability and real-time data integrity. Our local institutional review board has reviewed the amended consent forms and determined them to be compliant with federal regulations for this multi-site collaboration.

We commit to participating in all required site initiation visits, periodic monitoring reviews, and adverse event reporting cycles. The site’s principal investigator will coordinate closely with Dr. Saoirse Achterberg, Associate Professor of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine at Ashgrove, and Dr. Vikram Ostrowski-Baird, Director of the Clinical Trials Biostatistics Unit, to ensure alignment on primary and secondary endpoints.

Furthermore, we acknowledge the oversight role of Harbrace Clinical Monitoring, led by Dr. Ilse Nakagawa-Reilly, in maintaining regulatory compliance throughout the five-year study period. Our team is prepared to meet the enrollment targets set forth in the solicitation PA-25-305, leveraging our existing patient population and referral networks to facilitate timely recruitment.

## Administrative and Financial Commitment

Calloway Valley Health accepts the financial terms associated with its participation as outlined in the collaborative agreement. We confirm that our indirect cost rate of 62.5% on Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC) is currently active and compliant with federal guidelines. Our office of sponsored programs will manage all budget modifications, subaward reporting, and fringe benefit calculations at an agreed-upon rate of 31.2%.

This collaboration represents a significant opportunity to advance evidence-based practices in critical care. We are fully committed to the success of the SMSM trial and look forward to contributing our clinical expertise and institutional resources to this important national effort.
