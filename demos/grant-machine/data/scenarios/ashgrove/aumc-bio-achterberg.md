## Biographical Sketch

**Position:** Associate Professor of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine
**Institution:** Ashgrove University Medical Center

Dr. Saoirse Achterberg serves as an Associate Professor of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine at Ashgrove University Medical Center, a leading academic medical centre and affiliated university health system in the Mid-Atlantic region. With a robust clinical trial leadership record, Dr. Achterberg specializes in the optimization of care protocols for critically ill patients, particularly regarding early mobilization and sedation minimization strategies. Her work bridges clinical practice with rigorous research methodology, leveraging Ashgrove’s extensive infrastructure, which includes 1,140 hospital beds, 980 faculty physicians, and an annual research expenditure of $268 million.

Dr. Achterberg’s prior experience demonstrates her capacity to manage complex multi-site trials effectively. She is currently the Principal Investigator for the NIH National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute-funded award (ID: AUMC-FICT-R01-2021), titled "Early Mobilization Protocols in Mechanically Ventilated Patients." This five-year study, running from July 1, 2021, to June 30, 2026, has enrolled 288 participants against a target of 450 across four activated sites. Despite protocol deviations reported during the trial, the project has generated six peer-reviewed publications and remains on track for primary completion by June 30, 2025, with results due by June 30, 2026. This track record establishes her credibility in managing large-scale clinical investigations within a four-hospital system context.

For the proposed "Stepped Mobilization and Sedation Minimization in Critical Illness" (SMSM) trial, Dr. Achterberg will lead the study design and execution, working in close collaboration with Dr. Vikram Ostrowski-Baird, Director of the Clinical Trials Biostatistics Unit at Ashgrove. The SMSM project is aligned with NIH solicitation PA-25-305 (Parent R01 Clinical Trial Required) and will operate under Track 2 for a duration of five years, commencing July 1, 2027. Dr. Achterberg has secured formal letters of collaboration from Wrenfield Regional Hospital (dated August 9, 2026) and Calloway Valley Health (dated August 21, 2026), ensuring the necessary institutional support for multi-site activation beyond Ashgrove’s immediate footprint.
