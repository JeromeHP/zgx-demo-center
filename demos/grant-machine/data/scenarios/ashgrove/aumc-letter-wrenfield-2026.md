## Letter of Collaboration

**Date:** August 9, 2026

**To:**
Dr. Saoirse Achterberg
Associate Professor of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine
Ashgrove University Medical Center
Mid-Atlantic Region

**From:**
The Office of Clinical Research Administration
Wrenfield Regional Hospital

**Re:** Commitment to Participation in the Proposed Trial: Stepped Mobilization and Sedation Minimization in Critical Illness (SMSM)

This letter serves to formally confirm Wrenfield Regional Hospital’s commitment to serve as an active participating site for the proposed NIH Research Project Grant (Parent R01 Clinical Trial Required), solicitation PA-25-305. We have reviewed the study protocol, budget justification, and operational requirements for the SMSM project and are pleased to offer our full institutional support for site activation and clinical trial execution.

### Site Activation and Infrastructure

Wrenfield Regional Hospital is prepared to activate as a participating site immediately upon grant award notification, with an anticipated start date of July 1, 2027, aligning with the proposed project timeline. Our facility possesses the necessary critical care infrastructure, including intensive care unit beds and specialized respiratory therapy staff, to support the rigorous monitoring requirements of the Stepped Mobilization and Sedation Minimization protocol. We confirm that our institutional review board (IRB) has the capacity to expedite the initial ethics review process to ensure no delays in patient recruitment relative to the project start date.

### Local Coordination and Personnel Support

To ensure seamless integration with Ashgrove University Medical Center’s lead investigators, specifically Dr. Saoirse Achterberg and Co-Principal Investigator Dr. Vikram Ostrowski-Baird, Wrenfield Regional Hospital will assign a dedicated local clinical coordinator. This individual will be responsible for daily protocol adherence, data entry into the centralized electronic data capture system, and liaison with the Harbrace Clinical Monitoring team led by Dr. Ilse Nakagawa-Reilly. We commit to providing regular progress reports and facilitating remote monitoring visits as required by the grant terms.

### Projected Enrolment

Based on our current patient volume in the pulmonary and critical care divisions, Wrenfield Regional Hospital projects an enrolment of **35 eligible participants** over the five-year duration of the SMSM trial. This contribution is consistent with the multi-site recruitment strategy outlined in the application, supporting the overall target of 450 participants across all seven planned sites. We anticipate beginning screening within the first three months of site activation and aim to reach our enrolment goal by mid-year 2031.

### Institutional Commitment

Wrenfield Regional Hospital affirms that it will provide all necessary administrative support, including access to medical records for audit purposes, and will adhere to the data safety monitoring plans established by the principal investigator. We recognize the importance of this study in advancing early mobilization protocols for mechanically ventilated patients, building upon prior successful collaborations such as the NIH NHLBI-funded project AUMC-FICT-R01-2021.

We look forward to a productive partnership with Ashgrove University Medical Center and are confident that our combined efforts will yield significant clinical insights into sedation minimization strategies in critical illness.

Sincerely,

*Margaret Thorne*
