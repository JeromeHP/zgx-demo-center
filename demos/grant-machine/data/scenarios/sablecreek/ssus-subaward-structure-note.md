## Consortium Governance and Leadership Structure

The SSI project operates under a unified consortium model led by the Sablecreek State University System (Sablecreek System), a public university system office serving four campuses across the Northern plains. The System Office serves as the lead applicant and grantee, providing central administrative oversight while leveraging the distinct research strengths of its member institutions: Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend; Sablecreek State University — Pellamy; Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls; and Sablecreek Community and Technical College — Ondrak.

Executive leadership for the consortium is vested in Dr. Augustin Rieckhoff, System Vice Chancellor for Research, who serves as Principal Investigator (PI). Dr. Rieckhoff holds primary responsibility for overall project management, fiscal compliance, and strategic alignment with NSF EPSCoR requirements. Day-to-day operational coordination across the multi-campus network is managed by Dr. Marlowe Sandoval-Teague, Campus Research Lead at Grier Falls, who serves as Co-Principal Investigator (Co-PI). The administrative backbone of the consortium is provided by Delphine Okonkwo-Barr, System Director of Sponsored Programs, who oversees budget execution, subaward monitoring, and indirect cost recovery processes.

## Scope of Work and Campus Responsibilities

The consortium structure distributes specific research and infrastructure development tasks across participating entities to maximize regional coverage and technical expertise. While the previous EPSCOR award (SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021) successfully engaged three campuses, this new initiative expands participation to all four system campuses, building upon the prior outcomes of 47 faculty members engaged and 22 graduate students supported.

Harlow Bend and Grier Falls serve as the primary hubs for instrument deployment and data synthesis. Grier Falls, under the direction of Co-PI Dr. Sandoval-Teague, hosts the central coordination unit and leads the development of shared sensing protocols. Harlow Bend contributes specialized agricultural engineering resources. Pellamy focuses on soil science integration and field trial management, while Ondrak provides community engagement frameworks and technical training for regional stakeholders. External evaluation of project outcomes is conducted by Ridgeline Systems Evaluation, led by Dr. Theodora Vasquez-Lund, ensuring objective assessment of the shared sensing infrastructure’s impact on working lands across the region.

## Financial Structure and Subaward Administration

Financially, the SSI project requests a total of $9,644,873 over four years (2027-2031), with cost-share commitments of $250,000. The budget is allocated through subawards issued by the System Office to each participating campus. Each subaward includes a defined scope of work aligned with the project’s track_2 objectives and is governed by a formal agreement that delineates deliverables, reporting requirements, and financial responsibilities.

The fringe benefit rate applied across all personnel costs is 30.4%, pursuant to the SSUS Rate Agreement for FY2026. This rate ensures consistent compensation structures for staff employed by different campuses within the consortium.

## Facilities and Administrative (F&A) Cost Treatment

A critical component of the consortium’s financial integrity is the explicit treatment of Facilities and Administrative (F&A) costs, also known as indirect costs. The Sablecreek System has negotiated a composite indirect cost rate of 47.0% on Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC). This rate applies uniformly to all subawards issued within the SSI project.

Specifically:
1.  **Levied on Subaward Budgets:** The 47.0% F&A rate is calculated and charged to each campus subaward based on its respective MTDC. This ensures that the System Office recovers a proportionate share of institutional overhead costs associated with supporting the research infrastructure, including administrative services, library resources, and facility maintenance.
2.  **Uniform Application:** The indirect rate is applied consistently across all four campuses (Harlow Bend, Pellamy, Grier Falls, and Ondrak) regardless of their individual prior indirect cost rates or campus-specific overhead structures. This uniformity simplifies financial administration and ensures equitable treatment of consortium partners.
3.  **Compliance:** All F&A calculations adhere to the terms of the approved SSUS Rate Agreement for FY2026 and NSF regulations regarding allowable indirect costs. The System Office retains full authority over the distribution and retention of F&A revenues to support system-wide research infrastructure initiatives.

This subaward structure ensures that the SSI project maintains financial transparency, equitable resource distribution, and strict compliance with federal sponsorship requirements while fostering a collaborative environment across the Sablecreek State University System.
