## Preamble and Authority

This Negotiated Indirect Cost and Fringe Benefit Rate Agreement (the "Agreement") establishes the allowable rates for calculating fringe benefits and indirect costs associated with sponsored projects administered under the Sablecreek State University System (Sablecreek System). This document serves as the definitive reference for budget preparation, financial monitoring, and cost allocation for all federal and non-federal awards initiated on or after the effective date of FY2026. The Sablecreek System, a public university system office governing four campuses across the northern plains, including Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend, Sablecreek State University — Pellamy, Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls, and Sablecreek Community and Technical College — Ondrak, hereby certifies that the rates detailed herein have been negotiated in accordance with applicable federal regulations and institutional policy.

## Scope and Applicability

These rates apply to all modified total direct costs (MTDC) incurred by the System’s member campuses during Fiscal Year 2026. The agreement covers the full spectrum of research activities supported by external funding, ensuring consistency across the system’s annual research expenditure base, which currently stands at $96,000,000 USD. This standardization is critical for maintaining financial integrity across the system’s 47,500-student enrollment and its extensive collaborative networks, such as those established through prior awards like SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021.

## Fringe Benefit Rate

The negotiated fringe benefit rate for FY2026 is set at **30.4%** of modified total direct costs. This rate encompasses all personnel-related expenses, including but not limited to health insurance, retirement contributions, payroll taxes, and other mandatory benefits associated with salaries and wages. The application of this uniform rate ensures equitable treatment of staff across all four campuses, from the research-intensive environments at Grier Falls to the community-focused initiatives at Ondrak. Budgets prepared for new solicitations, such as the upcoming NSF 25-523 E-CORE track, must utilize this specific percentage when calculating personnel burden costs.

## Indirect Cost Rate

The on-campus organized research indirect cost rate is established at **47.0%** of modified total direct costs (MTDC). This rate reflects the proportionate share of institutional facilities and administrative expenses required to support sponsored projects. MTDC is defined as all salaries and wages, fringe benefits, materials, supplies, services, contracted costs, travel, and subawards up to the first $25,000 of each subaward or subcontract. Items such as equipment, capital expenditures, charges for patient care, tuition waivers, scholarships, fellowships, and participant support costs are excluded from the MTDC base.

This 47.0% rate is applicable to all on-campus research activities conducted at the System’s headquarters and its four member institutions. It is particularly relevant for projects requiring significant infrastructure utilization, such as the Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System (SSI), which seeks to leverage the system’s existing capabilities built upon the outcomes of the previous NSF EPSCoR award. The indirect cost recovery supports essential overhead functions, including library resources, information technology services, and facility maintenance, which are vital for sustaining the system’s $96,000,000 annual research expenditure.
