## Institutional Cost Share Commitment Overview

The Sablecreek State University System (Sablecreek System) is pleased to present a voluntary institutional cost share commitment of $250,000 over the four-year project period for the proposed initiative, *Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System* (SSI). This contribution is offered to strengthen the overall proposal and demonstrate the system’s deep institutional investment in advancing research infrastructure across our service area. The total committed amount of $250,000 equates to an annual commitment of $62,500, allocated evenly across fiscal years 1 through 4.

This cost share reflects the collective will of the System Office and its four member campuses to support the strategic goals of the EPSCoR Collaborations for Optimizing Research Ecosystems (E-CORE) program. By providing these resources, Sablecreek System underscores its commitment to fostering collaborative research environments that leverage shared facilities and interdisciplinary expertise. The funds are intended to offset indirect costs, support key personnel time, and enhance the sustainability of the proposed sensing infrastructure beyond the duration of federal funding.
