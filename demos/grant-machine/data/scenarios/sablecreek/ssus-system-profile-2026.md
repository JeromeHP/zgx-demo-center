## System Overview and Governance

The Sablecreek State University System (Sablecreek System) is a public higher education entity serving the Northern plains region through four distinct campuses. As an institution dedicated to expanding educational access and research capacity, the System maintains a total enrollment of 47,500 students. The administrative structure is anchored by the System Office, which provides centralized oversight while allowing member institutions to maintain specialized academic missions. Dr. Ingrid Velásquez-Moor serves as the Chancellor of the Sablecreek System, providing strategic leadership across the network.

The System comprises four member campuses, each contributing to the collective educational and research landscape:
*   **Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend**: The flagship liberal arts and sciences institution within the System.
*   **Sablecreek State University — Pellamy**: A comprehensive university focused on regional workforce development and applied sciences.
*   **Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls**: A technical institute emphasizing engineering, technology, and industrial partnerships.
*   **Sablecreek Community and Technical College — Ondrak**: An open-access college providing foundational education and vocational training.
