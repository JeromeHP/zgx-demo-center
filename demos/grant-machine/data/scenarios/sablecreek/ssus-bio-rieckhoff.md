## Personnel and Leadership Structure

Dr. Augustin Rieckhoff serves as the Principal Investigator for this proposal in his capacity as System Vice Chancellor for Research at Sablecreek State University System (Sablecreek System). As a public university system office overseeing four campuses across the northern plains, the Sablecreek System manages an annual research expenditure of $96 million and supports an enrollment of 47,500 students. ## Research Background and Institutional Context

The SSI project builds directly upon the success of prior infrastructure development within the Sablecreek System. Dr. Rieckhoff previously led the Statewide Research Infrastructure for Precision Agriculture (SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021), funded by NSF EPSCoR from September 1, 2021, through August 31, 2026, with a total award amount of $3.2 million. That initiative successfully engaged 47 faculty members across three participating campuses, deployed six shared instruments, and supported 22 graduate students. Crucially, it generated five external follow-on awards, demonstrating the system’s capacity to leverage initial federal investment into sustained research ecosystems.

The SSI project expands this legacy by establishing a unified sensing network across all four member campuses: Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend; Sablecreek State University — Pellamy; Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls; and Sablecreek Community and Technical College — Ondrak. This expansion addresses the geographic dispersion of agricultural research needs in the northern plains, a service area previously only partially covered by prior infrastructure efforts.
