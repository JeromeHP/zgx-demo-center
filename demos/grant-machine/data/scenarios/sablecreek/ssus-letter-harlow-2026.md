## Letter of Commitment — Harlow Bend Campus

**To:** The National Science Foundation
**Program:** EPSCoR Collaborations for Optimizing Research Ecosystems (E-CORE)
**Solicitation:** NSF 25-523
**Project Title:** Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System (SSI)
**Date:** August 7, 2026

This letter serves as a formal expression of commitment from Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend to support the proposed project titled "Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System." As a core member campus within the Sablecreek State University System, we recognize the critical importance of this initiative in advancing precision agriculture and strengthening regional research infrastructure. The SSI project aligns directly with our institutional strategic goals to enhance collaborative research capabilities and support graduate education in the Northern Plains region.

### Personnel Commitments

Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend is pleased to commit essential personnel to the successful execution of this four-year initiative, beginning September 1, 2027. We designate the following key individuals for dedicated service:

*   **Dr. Augustin Rieckhoff**, System Vice Chancellor for Research, will provide high-level oversight and strategic guidance. His involvement ensures alignment with system-wide priorities and facilitates inter-campus coordination across our four campuses.
*   **Dr. Marlowe Sandoval-Teague**, Campus Research Lead at Grier Falls (noting cross-system collaboration), will assist in technical integration efforts, leveraging his experience from prior NSF EPSCoR-funded infrastructure projects to ensure seamless adoption of shared sensing technologies.
*   **Delphine Okonkwo-Barr**, System Director of Sponsored Programs, will manage all administrative aspects of the award, including compliance, reporting, and financial oversight, ensuring adherence to NSF regulations and institutional policies.

Additionally, faculty members from Harlow Bend’s College of Agricultural Sciences will participate in the deployment and calibration of shared instruments, contributing expertise in field-based data collection and agronomic analysis.

### Facilities and Resources

Harlow Bend Campus commits to providing the following facilities and resources:

*   **Laboratory Space:** Dedicated laboratory space within the Center for Agricultural Innovation will be made available for sensor calibration and data processing activities.
*   **Field Research Plots:** Access to our 120-acre experimental farm, including equipped irrigation zones and soil monitoring stations, will support field validation of sensing technologies.
*   **Computing Infrastructure:** High-performance computing clusters in the Harlow Bend Data Center will be allocated for data storage and analytical processing.
*   **Cost Share:** The campus contributes a cost share of $250,000 toward the total project budget, covering equipment maintenance, personnel support, and operational expenses.

### Prior Experience and Collaboration

Building on the success of our prior award, SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021 (Statewide Research Infrastructure for Precision Agriculture), which engaged 47 faculty members and supported 22 graduate students across three campuses, Harlow Bend is prepared to expand this collaborative model. We anticipate that the SSI project will further engage our faculty and students, fostering new partnerships with external agencies such as the Northern Plains Agricultural Commission.

We are confident that this collaboration will significantly enhance research capacity across the Sablecreek System and deliver tangible benefits to working lands in the Northern Plains.
