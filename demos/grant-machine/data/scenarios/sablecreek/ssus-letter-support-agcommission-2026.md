## Letter of Support

**Date:** August 26, 2026

**To:** The National Science Foundation EPSCoR Program
**Re:** NSF Solicitation 25-523 – E-CORE Program
**Project:** Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System (SSI)
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Augustin Rieckhoff, System Vice Chancellor for Research

The Northern Plains Agricultural Commission hereby provides its enthusiastic letter of support for the SSI project proposed by the Sablecreek State University System. As a key stakeholder in the regional agricultural economy, our organization recognizes the critical need for advanced technological integration to sustain and improve the productivity of working lands across our four-campus service area.

The proposed initiative represents a vital next step following the successful completion of the prior statewide effort, *Statewide Research Infrastructure for Precision Agriculture* (NSF EPSCoR, SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021). That previous award, which concluded on August 31, 2026, laid essential groundwork by engaging forty-seven faculty members across three participating campuses and deploying six shared instruments. The SSI project builds directly upon this legacy, aiming to extend these benefits to all four member institutions: Sablecreek State University in Harlow Bend, Sablecreek State University in Pellamy, Sablecreek State Polytechnic in Grier Falls, and Sablecreek Community and Technical College in Ondrak.

We are particularly encouraged by the project’s focus on deploying shared sensing infrastructure over a four-year period starting September 1, 2027. This timeline aligns perfectly with our strategic goals for modernizing regional agricultural practices. The involvement of Dr. Marlowe Sandoval-Teague as Campus Research Lead at Grier Falls ensures that local expertise will guide the implementation of these technologies where they are most needed. Furthermore, the commitment of $250,000 in cost share by the System demonstrates a strong institutional dedication to the project’s success and sustainability.

The Northern Plains Agricultural Commission views the SSI project not merely as an academic exercise, but as a practical tool for regional producers. By enhancing data collection and analysis capabilities, this infrastructure will empower farmers and ranchers with real-time insights, leading to more efficient resource use and improved crop yields. We believe that the collaboration between the System’s research leadership and our commission’s network of producers will create a robust ecosystem for agricultural innovation.

Please note that while we strongly endorse the scientific merit and regional value of this proposal, this letter serves as an expression of support only. The Northern Plains Agricultural Commission does not commit any personnel, data, facilities, or other resources to this project through this document. We do not view this as a formal collaboration but rather as a testament to our confidence in the SSI team’s ability to deliver transformative outcomes for the northern plains region.

We look forward to seeing the results of this endeavor and hope that the National Science Foundation will approve this request to continue strengthening the research infrastructure that supports our regional agricultural community.

Sincerely,

*Eleanor Voss*
Commissioner
Northern Plains Agricultural Commission
