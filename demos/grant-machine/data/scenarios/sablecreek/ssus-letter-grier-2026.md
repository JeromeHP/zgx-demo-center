## Letter of Commitment from Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls Campus

**To:** National Science Foundation, EPSCoR Program
**Re:** Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System (SSI) | Solicitation NSF 25-523
**Date:** August 15, 2026

The administration of Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls Campus hereby confirms its formal commitment to participate in the proposed project, *Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System* (SSI). As a core member institution within the Sablecreek State University System, Grier Falls recognizes that robust, collaborative research infrastructure is essential to advancing our collective mission in precision agriculture and environmental monitoring. This letter outlines our specific contributions, particularly regarding sensing instrumentation, and affirms our alignment with the project’s strategic goals.

### Institutional Context and Prior Success

The Sablecreek System, a public university system serving the northern plains with four campuses and an annual research expenditure of $96,000,000, has a proven track record of leveraging federal support for regional infrastructure development. Our participation in the prior NSF EPSCoR-funded award, *Statewide Research Infrastructure for Precision Agriculture* (SSUS-FICT-EPSCOR-2021), demonstrated the efficacy of this collaborative model. That three-year initiative engaged 47 faculty members and supported 22 graduate students across three participating campuses, deploying six shared instruments and resulting in five external follow-on awards. Building on this foundation, Grier Falls Campus is prepared to expand our technical contributions under the new SSI framework.

### Sensing Instrumentation Contribution

Grier Falls Campus, as a polytechnic institution, will serve as the primary hub for the deployment and maintenance of advanced sensing instrumentation critical to the SSI project. Our commitment includes:

1.  **Provision of Hardware:** The campus will allocate resources to procure and install high-fidelity soil moisture sensors, atmospheric data loggers, and multispectral imaging units at designated field sites across the system’s service area. These instruments will complement the existing infrastructure established under the prior EPSCoR award.
2.  **Technical Maintenance and Calibration:** Dedicated engineering staff within the Grier Falls campus will be assigned to ensure the continuous operation and calibration of these sensing networks. This includes quarterly maintenance protocols and rapid response repair services to minimize data gaps, ensuring the integrity of the long-term monitoring dataset.
3.  **Data Integration Infrastructure:** The campus will contribute its computational resources and expertise in data management to integrate sensor outputs into a unified system-wide platform. This effort aligns with the project’s objective of creating a cohesive research ecosystem for working lands.

### Administrative and Financial Commitment

This commitment is supported by the endorsement of Dr. Marlowe Sandoval-Teague, Campus Research Lead at Grier Falls, and is coordinated through the System Director of Sponsored Programs, Delphine Okonkwo-Barr. The SSI project, spanning four years from September 1, 2027, to August 31, 2031, seeks a total of $9,644,873 in direct costs. Grier Falls Campus acknowledges the system’s commitment of $250,000 in cost share, which will be utilized to offset a portion of the instrumentation and personnel costs associated with this project.

We are confident that the technical expertise and institutional support provided by Sablecreek State Polytechnic — Grier Falls Campus will significantly enhance the success of the Shared Sensing Infrastructure for Working Lands Across the System. We look forward to collaborating with our partners, including Sablecreek State University — Harlow Bend and the Northern Plains Agricultural Commission, to achieve the project’s objectives.
