# Sample image credits

Every photo is openly licensed (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public domain; never NC or ND) and publicly
available at the source linked below. They come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr (via Openverse).
All were **resized to 1024 px on the long edge**; nothing else was changed. CC BY-SA images remain under
their original licence. `vehicles.json` holds the same data, and the demo shows each photo's credit
wherever the photo is shown.

Each vehicle has 2-4 views of the **same physical car**, checked by eye (paint, wheels, plates, trim),
as were brands and models. Every vehicle is a Stellantis brand.
